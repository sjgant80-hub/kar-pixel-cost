The kestrel hovered over the meadow, wings beating fast, eyes fixed on the grass below. A vole moved through the grass, unaware of the kestrel above. The kestrel folded its wings and dropped. The vole never saw the kestrel. Grass bent where the kestrel struck. The meadow went quiet again, the kestrel already rising with the vole, wings beating toward a fence post at the meadow's edge.
